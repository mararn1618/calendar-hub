# How should Day, Week and Next X Days scroll?

Type: prototype
Label: wayfinder:prototype
Status: open

## Question

When looking ahead from a workday, how should the date columns and hours move under a trackpad or mouse? Use a small example with all-day items, overlapping appointments and timed tasks. Establish which day opens first, what starts a week, how X is chosen, what stays in view while scrolling, and how switching views or returning to Today affects the position. Ask which other views, if any, are essential.
