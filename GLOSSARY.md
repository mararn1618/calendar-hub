# Calendar Hub

## Language

**TickTick task**

A to-do kept in TickTick. It can have a day, a time slot and a completion state. Checking it off in Calendar Hub means completing the original task in TickTick.

**TickTick calendar**

A local view of task lists from one TickTick account. The account starts as one calendar. It can also be divided by TickTick folder or into calendars the user names.

**TickTick list and folder**

A list holds the original tasks. A folder in TickTick groups lists and has its own place among the account's loose lists. These positions determine the order of the list picker.

**Calendar event**

An appointment on a Google or Exchange calendar. It can occupy a time slot or span whole days.

**BMW calendar**

The work calendar kept in Exchange. The existing workaround makes its meetings visible through a Mac calendar or through Google Calendar.

**TickTick copy calendar**

The separate Google calendar containing event copies of TickTick tasks. The original tasks belong to TickTick.

**Hide in Calendar Hub**

A local visibility choice for an entry. The appointment stays on its source calendar and its guests receive no change.

**Next X Days**

A view of consecutive dates. X is the number of days shown.
